Raja Labs

Painting potential landscapes on an atomically thin canvas

Molecular Foundry  ·  Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

Light, electrons & atomically thin matter

We are a research group based at the Molecular Foundry and the Kavli Energy NanoScience Institute at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, on the hill just minutes up from the UC Berkeley campus. Our own research drives what we build and learn, and that in turn lets us help our collaborators and Foundry users push the boundaries of their science.

At the nanoscale, a material’s surroundings become part of it. A neighboring layer, a single defect, or a slight twist can shift energy levels and trap excitations. These same features steer how light, heat, charge, and spin carry energy and information, and how they convert from one form to another. We treat these interfaces and local environments as design tools, building and tuning nanoscale quantum materials to create new electronic and optical landscapes. We then use photons and electrons to watch what happens inside them.

We are also building AI co-scientists and closed-loop workflows that connect theory, simulation, and experiment. Our goal is to speed up the discovery of new functionality in quantum materials for energy-efficient electronics and quantum technologies.
