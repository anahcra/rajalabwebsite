Getting Started

Getting Started Lab Life Values FAQ

We welcome scientists who combine creativity and curiosity with resilience and hard work. Together, we make materials, build instrumentation and use facilities to characterize matter at its smallest and fastest scales.

Ways to join

Postdocs

Postdoc positions are available through Berkeley Lab.

You can also be nominated to work with Archana through the Kavli ENSI Heising-Simons Junior Fellowship ↗ (opens in a new tab). It is highly competitive, and nominations typically close in September.

Graduate students

PhD students can carry out part of their thesis research with us through the DOE Office of Science Graduate Student Research (SCGSR) program ↗ (opens in a new tab).

UC Berkeley students co-advised by Archana can also apply for the Kavli ENSI Graduate Student Fellowship ↗ (opens in a new tab).

Undergraduates

We welcome undergraduates who want hands-on research experience. Get in touch to ask about current projects.

You can also come through one of the Berkeley Lab undergraduate programs ↗ (opens in a new tab), such as the DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internship (SULI).

Getting started in the lab

Whether you join the group or come as a Molecular Foundry user, you follow the same path before working on our lasers, cryostats and microscopes: get approved, get on site, complete safety training, then train hands-on on each instrument until you are authorized to work on your own.

  1. Get approved

    Group members join through a Berkeley Lab appointment (see Ways to join). External researchers use our instruments through a peer-reviewed Molecular Foundry user proposal ↗ (opens in a new tab); please email us before applying so we can discuss feasibility and scope.

  2. Get on site

    Request your Berkeley Lab badge and affiliate appointment in the Foundry User Portal ↗ (opens in a new tab) 1 to 2 months before you arrive. Complete General Employee Radiation Training (EHS0470) before your badge appointment. See Site Access ↗ (opens in a new tab) for details.

  3. Complete safety training

    Your required courses appear in the User Portal and the Lab’s training system once your proposal or appointment is active.

    TrainingWho needs it
    Berkeley Lab core online courses, including GERT (EHS0470)Everyone
    MFD-0012 New User Orientation (30 min, daily)Everyone working on site
    MFD-0010 ISM Principles and Case StudiesEveryone doing lab work
    EHS 0302 Laser SafetyAnyone working with our lasers
    EHS 0304 Laser AlignmentAnyone who aligns laser beams
    EHS 0604 Hazardous Waste, then MFD-0015 Waste BriefingAnyone who generates chemical waste, for example during sample preparation

  4. Facility onboarding

    Your contact in our group walks you through the lab, its hazards and emergency procedures, and adds you to the work authorizations for the instruments you will use.

  5. Hands-on instrument training

    A trained super-user teaches you each instrument from its written procedure. You first work under supervision, then are authorized to work independently once you have shown you can run the instrument safely. Laser work after hours needs separate approval.

Questions? Email Archana at araja@lbl.gov. The Foundry’s Orientation and Training ↗ (opens in a new tab) page has the full official process.
