Privacy

We count visits to this website to learn where our readers are and how they find us. Here is what that involves.

  • What a visit is. One browser tab opening the site. Reading more pages in the same tab doesn’t add visits. There are no cookies or visitor IDs, so returning visitors aren’t told apart and no one is followed from one visit to the next.
  • What’s recorded. The approximate city, browser, operating system, device type, language, time zone, the site a visitor came from, and the first page they opened. No names, email addresses or IP addresses are stored.
  • Who else sees it. To find the city, the visitor’s browser asks a free location service (ipwho.is, or ipapi.co or geojs.io if it’s unavailable), which sees the IP address. Visits are stored in a database hosted by Supabase.
  • What this page shows. Totals and breakdowns. Recent visits show only the country and roughly when. Busiest times use each visitor’s own time zone; everything else is in yours.
  • Opting out. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control aren’t counted, and neither are known bots or visits to this page.
